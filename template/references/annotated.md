# Annotated bibliography

For each source, record:

- the exact result relevant to the open question;
- whether it states the problem as open, solves a case, improves a bound, or
  changes the formulation;
- a DOI, arXiv ID, paper ID, and LKM node ID when available;
- the publication date and the date the source was checked.

Do not replace exact evidence with a generic paper summary.
